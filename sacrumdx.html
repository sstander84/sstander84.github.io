Sacral findings follow the Sacrum II criteria (Sandhouse); pelvic findings follow Pelvis II (Reindel), where anterior rotation is ASIS inferior and PSIS superior on the affected side, and so on. Landmark findings are relative, so a case may describe them from either side — the test of laterality is what names the side. Pubic shears are included because they appear in the OPP III question bank.
